Know your flock. Lead with clarity.

Our why

Most churches still track their people the way they did thirty years ago: paper registers, scattered spreadsheets, and a pastor's memory. When a member drifts quietly out the back door, no one notices until it's too late.

The problem

Congregational care doesn't scale past a few dozen people without a system, and most churches have never had one built for them.

How we help

Church Pilot turns attendance, follow-up, and giving into one clear view, so leaders spend less time hunting for information and more time with people.

We help churches understand their congregation through simple, purpose-built software.

Every congregation has a story worth knowing, person by person.

Meet Church Pilot

Our flagship product gives leadership teams a live view of attendance trends, member follow-up, and giving, replacing the spreadsheet sprawl with one dashboard everyone trusts.
