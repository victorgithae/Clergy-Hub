Church Pilot

Church Pilot brings attendance, follow-up, and giving into one dashboard, so leadership teams always know where their congregation stands — and who needs a call this week.

View demo

Inside Church Pilot

Everything you need to shepherd your flock.

Built for modern ministry, Church Pilot combines member management, targeted communication, transparent finances, and AI-driven insights into one powerful platform.

People & Visitors

Know your congregation intimately. Track members, manage cell groups, and ensure no first-time visitor slips through the cracks.

  • Comprehensive member profiles
  • Dedicated visitor follow-up paths
  • Cell group & department management

Smart Communication

Engage the right people at the right time with segmented broadcasting and automated messaging sequences.

  • Bulk SMS & WhatsApp integration
  • Automated birthdays & reminders
  • Targeted group segmentation

Giving & Finance

Steward church finances with total transparency. Track every contribution, pledge, and project budget effortlessly.

  • Tithes, offerings & pledges
  • Income, expenses & budgeting
  • Downloadable financial reports

Church Pilot AI

Your intelligent ministry assistant. Use natural language queries to instantly generate insights about your congregation's health.

  • Identify members needing follow-up
  • Analyze giving & attendance patterns
  • Generate custom ministry reports

Events & Calendar

Keep your entire leadership team aligned. Plan special services, seminars, and ministry events from one central hub.

  • Centralized church calendar
  • Past & upcoming event tracking
  • Streamlined ministry scheduling

Insights & Analytics

Stop guessing and start leading with clarity. Visual dashboards summarize the holistic health and growth of your church.

  • Visual attendance metrics
  • Real-time financial tracking
  • Scheduled follow-up dashboards
