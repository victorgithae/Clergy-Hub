Our origins

Clergy Hub started with a stack of attendance registers and a question: why does software help every kind of organization know its people except the church? Our founding team had each, separately, watched a congregation lose someone quietly — not from a crisis of faith, but from being missed. We built the tool we wished existed.

Our approach

We spend a week embedded in a church before we design for it. Every feature ships to a handful of real congregations first, and only earns a wider release once pastors — not our own team — decide it's actually useful on a Sunday.

Mission

Equip clergy with clear, trustworthy tools so no one in their care is overlooked.

Vision

A world where every congregation, of any size, has the same clarity a Fortune 500 team has about its people.

Core values

Built with pastors, not just for them. Simple before clever. Trust earned in the details.
